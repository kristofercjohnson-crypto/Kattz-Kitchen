ABOUT THE CREATOR

Katt Johnz

Katt Johnz created Get Saucy as a welcoming place for home cooks, chefs, food trucks, caterers, and local restaurants to share food, recipes, and stories. Inspired by Mississippi flavor and a love of good food, Get Saucy celebrates the people behind every plate.
